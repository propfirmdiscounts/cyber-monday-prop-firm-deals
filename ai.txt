# Cyber Monday - PropFirmDiscount.com

Cyber Monday prop firm deals and the discount codes of the firms that ran them.

## Dataset

25 deals from 20 prop firms, newest 2025-12-16.
Each row states the deal, its dates, the firm's discount code, and where to redeem it.
